Expanding Horizons in Physics in Sudan II

Density Functional Theory — Methods and Applications

A Physics Without Frontiers initiative, in partnership with ICTP (Trieste, Italy)

  • 📅 27 July – 8 August 2026 | 🌐 Online
  • ⏳ Registration deadline: 20 June 2026
  • 🔗 Register here

Since April 2023, Sudan's universities and research institutions have faced devastating disruption. Yet science does not stop — and neither do we.
Building on the success of our 2025 PWF–Sudan initiative, Photon SCNSM and ICTP are proud to bring Sudanese students and early-career researchers a nine-day intensive workshop in Density Functional Theory (DFT) — one of the most powerful computational tools in modern materials science.
Through daily morning lectures and afternoon hands-on tutorials, participants will work directly with internationally recognized software tools (Quantum ESPRESSO, Python, ASE) and gain the foundations needed for independent research, PhD programmes, and international collaboration.

This year's speakers include:

  • 🎙 Richard Martin – University of Illinois & Stanford
  • 🎙 Natasa Stojic – ICTP, Italy
  • 🎙 Michele Pavanello – Rutgers University
  • 🎙 Andre Schleife – University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
  • 🎙 Iurii Timrov – Paul Scherrer Institut, Switzerland
  • 🎙 Christian Carbogno – Fritz Haber Institute, Berlin
  • 🎙 Omololu Akin-Ojo – University of Ibadan
  • …and more

Topics covered:
Quantum mechanics · Solid-state physics · Computational materials science · DFT fundamentals · Band structure calculations · DFT+U · Phonons · Machine learning in electronic structure

Who should apply?
Advanced undergraduates, MSc students, and early PhD researchers in physics, chemistry, materials science, or related fields — with basic exposure to quantum mechanics or solid-state physics.

All sessions will be recorded and shared in low-bandwidth formats. ICTP certificates will be awarded to participants who complete the full programme.
